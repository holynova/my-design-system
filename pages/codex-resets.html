07

Do's & don'ts

Do

  • Keep the cream-and-cocoa base intact.
  • Use one dominant status figure.
  • Give pastel colors stable metric roles.
  • Keep labels, dates, and axes in mono.

Don't

  • Don't add blurred shadows or glass panels.
  • Don't use Baloo 2 for long body copy.
  • Don't make every surface orange.
  • Don't rotate wrappers or nested text.
08

Responsive behavior

640px

The single compact breakpoint tightens page and card padding, reduces chart cells to 15px, shortens metric labels, stacks section headings, and keeps the three summary tiles visible side by side.

09

Agent prompt guide

Build a compact operational tracker with warm cream paper (#fff4dd), cocoa ink (#26201a), Baloo 2 display type, system-sans body copy, and mono metadata. Use 2px ink outlines and unblurred 3–6px offset shadows. Make one yellow-highlighted status figure dominant; reserve orange for active events and rose/sky for supporting metrics. Buttons physically depress by translating to their shadow offset. Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, blurred shadows, and extra accent colors.
